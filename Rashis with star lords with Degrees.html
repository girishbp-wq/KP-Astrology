In this illustration of the southern chart,

each house is color coded according to

its natural lord. Starting from the top left

house for example, you will see PISCES

colored lavendar. It is ruled by Jupiter, as

well as the sign of Sagittarius, also in lavendar.

Aries and Scorpio are in RED, ruled by Mars-

Taurus and Libra are in BLUE, ruled by Venus-

Gemini and Virgo are in GREEN, ruled by the sign of Mercury -

Cancer is in PINK, ruled by the Moon

Leo is in YELLOW, ruled by the Sun
